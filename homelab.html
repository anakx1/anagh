Homelab

The lab behind the portfolio

A KVM/virt-manager environment for RHEL administration, Ansible automation, SELinux, storage, and recovery practice. This is where the projects are built and broken.

KVM / virt-manager

Overview

The lab runs on an Ubuntu KVM/virt-manager host; the guests are RHEL-family. A three-node Ansible group — RHEL 10 control, with RHEL 9 and Rocky Linux 9 managed nodes — handles automation practice. Each lab is documented with the commands, the failure, and the fix.

I use snapshots before destructive work, keep a separate automation user on managed nodes, and test recovery from a console rather than relying on a working desktop.
